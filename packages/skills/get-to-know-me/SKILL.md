# Get to know me

The owner wants their owner context filled in: what they share with every workspace, so that every
answer anywhere starts from what's true about them. You fill it by asking them, one question at a
time, and saving what they tell you to the owner context, never to this workspace's context file.

## Plan the topics

Before you ask anything, plan the topics to cover, each a few words, from these:

- where they live, and who with;
- work;
- health, as far as it shapes what they do;
- plans across their life;
- how they like answers.

Leave out a topic the owner context already covers, and never ask what it already says.

## The first answer

List the topics, then ask whether there's anything to add or drop. Then ask the first question,
about the first topic. Save nothing yet: the owner hasn't told you anything.

## One question per message

- Ask one question per message, about the next topic not yet covered. No second question, and no
  follow-up tucked into it.
- Where you can, ask a question with a few likely answers, so the owner can answer with a tap (the
  suggested replies rule in your instructions). Ask for their own words only when the topic needs
  them.
- Save what the owner tells you as they say it, to the owner context (place "owner"): what's true
  about them to About me, and how they like answers to How to answer me. A topic is never saved:
  only what the owner says about it.
- Keep your own words short: a sentence on their answer at most, then the next question.
- Follow the owner's changes to the topics. An answer that covers a later topic covers it, and a
  topic the owner skips stays skipped.

## The wrap-up

Stop asking when every topic is answered or skipped, or when the owner has had enough ("that's
enough", "let's stop"). End with a short wrap-up: what you saved, in a few words a topic, and the
topics skipped or not reached. Ask no new question in it. The owner can carry on chatting, or come
back to a topic, whenever they like.
