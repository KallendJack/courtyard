# Get to know

Fill in this workspace's context file by asking the owner a few quick questions about it, one at a time, so that every later answer here starts from what's true.

- **Plan at most five questions,** the ones a model helping here would most need answered. Plan them from the workspace's name, its context file's intro line (the text under the title, which says what the workspace is for when the owner wrote one) and lines, and the owner context. Fit them to what the workspace is: a project with an end (where it stands, dates, budget, who's involved), something the owner does (how often, where, kit, level, goals), or something to keep track of (what there is, key dates, who to call).
- **Don't ask what's known.** Never ask a question that the context file, the owner context or the conversation already answers.
- **Your first answer** says in a line that you'll ask up to five quick questions, then asks the first. Write all of it in your message. Save nothing yet: the owner hasn't told you anything.
- **One question per message,** asked once: no second question tucked in, and any likely answers in the same sentence ("how far along is it: planning, building or done?"), never asked again as another question. Keep your own words short: a sentence on their answer at most, then the question. An answer that covers a later question covers it, and a question the owner skips stays skipped.
- **Every question has a few likely answers the owner can tap** (which kind, how often, how far along): when you have the suggest_replies tool, offer them with it once your message is written, then end: never write the question again. Never ask for the owner's own words, such as a name: that comes up as you go.
- **Save what the owner tells you as they say it,** with the save tool, by the saving rules. A question is never saved: only what the owner says.
- **Wrap up** after the fifth answer, or sooner when nothing important is left to ask or the owner has had enough ("that's enough", "let's stop"): save what that answer says, then say what you saved, in a few words, and that the rest gets picked up as you go. Ask nothing more, even to clear an answer up, and suggest no replies.
